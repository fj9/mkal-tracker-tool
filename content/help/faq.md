## What do start, work and end mean?

Start is the stitches on your needle before the row, end is the count after it (it matches your pattern), and work is how many stitches you actually work in the row. Progress and daily targets use work.

## Why is work different from end?

Bind-offs count the stitches you bind off, an I-cord bind-off counts three for each stitch, and short rows count only the stitches between the turns.

## How are the daily targets worked out?

Total stitches to work divided by your days. "Where to be" is the last row you would reach by the end of each day.

## Which version should I choose?

Pick the size or stitch option you are knitting. Each version keeps its own progress, so you can switch without losing your place.

## I knitted ahead, or started without the app.

Open Sections, find your row and choose "Tick up to here".

## I frogged or made a mistake.

Untick the rows; progress and targets update straight away.

## I need more or fewer days.

Change the start date or days on Plan. Only the targets change.

## Where are the pattern instructions?

Public versions of the app show row numbers, stitch counts and colours only. Keep your pattern PDF open for the instructions.

## Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on this device only. Add the app to your home screen, and use Back up in Settings to save a file you can restore later.

## Can I use it on two devices?

Not yet. Back up on one device and restore on the other; ticks made on both will not merge.

## My total is different from my own spreadsheet.

The app counts effort using the rules in Settings, so totals can differ by a few percent, but the row-by-row stitch counts match the pattern.

## The app says a clue has been updated.

The data was corrected. Your ticks are kept for every row that still exists; check the rows it lists.

## How do I add the app to my home screen?

iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: the browser menu, then Install app.
