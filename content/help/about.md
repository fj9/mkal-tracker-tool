Hi, I'm Freya, or Freya'd Knot. I'm a serial WestKnits MKAL knitter, and I'd rather spend an evening on formulas than lose my place in a chart. So for each MKAL I build a tracker, with row counts, stitch maths and a "where should I be by today?" timeline, plus a few interesting stats. This app is the latest version.

I like my knitting in two flavours: slightly too complicated for the thrill of the challenge, and mindless and portable for commutes. I usually have one of each on the go.

Off the needles, I studied computer science and archaeology at the University of Birmingham, where I co-ran the knitting society. Now I work in Data Engineering, so I spend my days organising data and my evenings organising stitches. It's the same instinct: I love a good spreadsheet, and I like a good system even more.

These trackers and stats are purely for fun. I'm not trying to sell anything. Knitting isn't a race, so please work at a pace that makes you happy.

I don't get the clues early and I mostly knit along as each one is released, so every effort is made to get the numbers right, but sometimes I correct them. I'm not a spreadsheet wizard, so I apologise for any rough edges.

The first version of my trackers was adapted from Anne Reimer's (plaidnuthatch) Starflake MKAL tracker, and I'm very grateful for her work.

Everything you tick stays on your device. I share the trackers and the MKAL stats on [Ravelry](https://www.ravelry.com/people/freyadknot) and [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/freyaj9/), and the code lives on [GitHub](https://github.com/fj9) if you'd like to take a look.
