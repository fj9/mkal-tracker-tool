## Pick your clue

Choose an MKAL, then the clue you are knitting. If a clue has versions, such as a size or stitch option, pick yours. Each version keeps its own progress.

## Make it yours

In Settings, choose the yarn colour for each colour code, for example Blue for MC. Your colours show up on Knit and Overview. You can also switch between light and dark mode there.

## Set your week

Choose your start date and how many days you have. The app shares the stitches evenly across the days and shows where you should be each day.

## Tick as you go

On Knit, tap Done after each row. If you have been knitting without the app, tap Check in and pick the last row you finished. The Overview tab shows every row at a glance, and ticking a row there ticks everything before it.
